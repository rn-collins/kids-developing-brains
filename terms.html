Use boundaries

Effective August 16, 2026.

This free educational tool helps an adult structure questions about one proposed use of generative AI involving children or teens. Its recommendation is a conservative, deterministic prompt for human review—not a product certification, diagnosis, developmental assessment, safety guarantee, or legal determination.

The tool is not medical, mental-health, educational, legal, privacy, cybersecurity, or procurement advice. It does not replace a caregiver, educator, clinician, privacy officer, school administrator, counsel, or other qualified reviewer.

Research on long-term developmental effects remains incomplete. Product behavior, terms, laws, policies, and official guidance can change. Users must inspect the actual product configuration, retrieve current primary materials, apply organizational rules, and revisit decisions when circumstances change.

Do not use the tool to make automated or unsupported decisions about a child’s health, safety, discipline, eligibility, disability, education, access, rights, or relationships. An accountable adult must remain responsible for consequential decisions.
